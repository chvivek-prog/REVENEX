# REVENEX — Phase 18 Revenue Impact Prioritization

## Purpose

Phase 18 answers:

> Where should the business focus first?

REVENEX evaluates multiple revenue opportunities and ranks
them according to:

- Exposure
- Expected recovery
- Remaining exposure
- Urgency
- Prediction confidence
- Evidence quality

## Output

Every opportunity receives:

- Impact score
- Impact level
- Recommended focus
- Explanation
- Evidence references

## Priority Levels

CRITICAL
→ Immediate human review

HIGH
→ Priority human review

MEDIUM
→ Scheduled review

LOW
→ Monitor

## Strategic Value

Phase 17 answers:

"What could happen under different scenarios?"

Phase 18 answers:

"Which opportunity deserves attention first?"

Together they move REVENEX toward:

Observe
→ Investigate
→ Predict
→ Simulate
→ Prioritize
→ Decide
