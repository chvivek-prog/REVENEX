# REVENEX — Phase 15 Revenue Opportunity Intelligence

## Purpose

Risk intelligence answers:

"Where can revenue be lost?"

Opportunity intelligence adds:

"Where is the most valuable recoverable revenue opportunity?"

## Inputs

The deterministic opportunity engine can use:

- Customer exposure
- Outstanding revenue
- Risk score
- Confidence
- Recoverable amount
- Recovery rate

## Outputs

Each opportunity contains:

- Opportunity ID
- Customer
- Exposure
- Recoverable amount
- Opportunity score
- Priority
- Reason
- Evidence references
- Governance state

## Priority

Opportunities are classified as:

- CRITICAL
- HIGH
- MEDIUM
- LOW

## Executive Value

This allows REVENEX to move from:

Revenue visibility
→ Revenue risk

toward:

Revenue visibility
→ Revenue risk
→ Recoverable opportunity
→ Prioritized human review
