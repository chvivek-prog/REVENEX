# REVENEX — 5–7 Minute Demo Script

## 1. Opening — 30 seconds

"REVENEX is an Autonomous Revenue Operating System.

Its operating loop is:

Observe → Understand → Predict → Simulate → Decide → Safely Act → Monitor Outcome → Learn → Audit.

The goal is to turn fragmented revenue and payment data into governed, explainable revenue decisions."

---

## 2. Show the Revenue Intelligence API — 60 seconds

Demonstrate the canonical revenue intelligence analysis.

Show:

- Revenue observations
- Invoice intelligence
- Payment intelligence
- Revenue graph
- Revenue leakage
- Anomalies
- Root causes
- Forecast
- Decision intelligence

Explain:

"REVENEX doesn't just report numbers. It connects the signals and determines what requires attention."

---

## 3. Show Investigation — 60 seconds

Demonstrate:

- Revenue leakage
- Anomaly detection
- Root-cause analysis
- Settlement variance
- Payment/invoice mismatches

Explain:

"The system moves from detecting a problem toward explaining why the problem exists."

## 4. Show Prediction and Simulation — 60 seconds

Show:

- Confidence
- Risk
- Scenario/simulation output

Explain:

"Before taking an action, REVENEX can evaluate the expected impact and uncertainty."
